Anthropic API key

KotoLens uses Claude to translate, add furigana, and explain grammar. Create a key at console.anthropic.com. The key is stored only on this device and sent only to api.anthropic.com. You pay Anthropic directly for usage.

Pronunciation

Voices come from Chrome and your operating system. If none are listed, install a Japanese or Korean voice in your OS speech settings.

Privacy

When you use KotoLens, the highlighted text, the sentence and paragraph around it, and the page title are sent to Anthropic's API to generate the explanation. Nothing is sent until you choose a KotoLens menu item. Flashcards and settings stay in Chrome storage on your device. KotoLens has no servers and no analytics.
